# 8. Missions & Contracts

Beyond free trading, the galaxy offers **contracts** — jobs that pay rewards and build your legend. Open the **Mission** screen to see what is available and what you have accepted. This chapter explains the four kinds of contracts.

---

## Types of missions

There are four main categories:

### 1. Ride (passenger transport)
You agree to carry a passenger from one system to another. The passenger occupies cargo space for the trip, and you are paid on arrival. Simple, safe, and a good way to earn while you travel a route you already planned.

### 2. Deliver (cargo delivery)
You are given (or asked to buy) a specific quantity of a good and deliver it to a target system. The required cargo is reserved in your hold. Deliver it to collect the reward. This is essentially a paid, guaranteed trade route.

### 3. Manhunt
A suspect is wanted in a particular system. You must travel there and confront them. Manhunts often turn into combat with a specific target ship. Success pays a bounty and boosts your reputation. Some manhunts begin as **patrol** missions that escalate.

### 4. Hero (police hero)
The authorities ask you to **destroy a number of enemy ships** (for example, a fleet of Mantis or Scorp raiders) in a given system. These are combat-heavy and dangerous, but the rewards and reputation gains are large. The more ships required, the bigger the payout.

---

## Accepting and tracking missions

- The Mission screen lists available contracts (filtered by type) and your active ones.
- Some missions require a minimum **police record** to accept (for example, clean-record jobs are not offered to criminals).
- Accepted missions are tracked: you can see what you must deliver, where you must go, and how many enemies remain.
- Reserved cargo (for deliveries) is locked in your hold so you cannot accidentally sell it.

---

## Rewards

Completing a mission pays **credits** and usually improves your **reputation** (chapter 9). Failing or canceling a mission can hurt your reputation slightly, so only take jobs you intend to finish.

Manhunts and hero missions are the fastest way to build a fearsome reputation (up to "Elite"), while rides and deliveries are steady, low-risk income for cautious players.

---

## Strategy

- Combine a **deliver** mission with your normal trade route to get paid twice for the same trip.
- Use **ride** missions to subsidize travel you were already doing.
- Save **manhunt** and **hero** missions for when your ship is well armed and shielded.
- Check the Mail screen too — some contract offers and updates arrive there.
