# 11. Options & Saving

This final chapter covers the settings you can tweak and, importantly, where your game is stored — so you never lose your progress by accident.

---

## Automatic saving

Your game saves itself to your **browser's local storage** as you play. There is no "save" button to remember. When you close the tab and reopen it later, you resume exactly where you left off — same galaxy, same ship, same credits.

A few things to know:

- Saves are **per browser and per device**. They do not follow you to a different computer or a different browser.
- Clearing your browser data (or using a "private"/incognito window that discards storage) will erase your save.
- Your commander profile, the galaxy, your ship, your missions, and your play time are all persisted automatically.

---

## Options menu

Press **Options** (the gear icon) to open the settings panel. You can adjust:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Auto Fullscreen** | Automatically enter fullscreen when the game starts. |
| **Show Framerate** | Display an FPS counter (mainly for troubleshooting). |
| **Hide Breaking News** | Suppress the rotating news headlines on the map. |
| **No Traders** | Automatically ignore trader encounters (fewer distractions). |
| **Disable Audio** | Turn all sound off. |
| **No Blur Effects** | Remove background blur (helps on slow devices). |
| **Music Volume** | Slider for the background music level. |
| **Resolution Scaling** | Lower this if the game runs slowly; raise it for sharper visuals. |
| **Encounter Rate** | Scale how often random encounters happen (lower = calmer travel, higher = more danger and opportunity). |

These are personal comfort settings; none of them change the core rules.

---

## New game and reset

From Options you can also:

- **New Game** — start fresh while keeping your settings. This erases your current progress, so the game asks you to confirm.
- **Reset** — wipe all progress *and* restore every setting to default.

Use these only when you truly want to begin again.

---

## Mobile and desktop

The game is designed to work on both. On a phone or tablet:

- The control buttons move to a bottom footer bar.
- The screen may ask you to rotate to portrait if you are holding it sideways (a reminder overlay appears); rotating back removes it.
- Touch gestures (tap, drag, pinch) replace mouse actions.

Your saves work the same on mobile as on desktop.

---

## Sound and music

The game has ambient music and sound effects (lasers, explosions, warp drives, and more). If anything is too loud or annoying, use **Disable Audio** or the **Music Volume** slider. Audio preferences are saved.
