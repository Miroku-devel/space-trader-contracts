# Space Trader — Contracts: Player Manual

Welcome, Commander. This is a complete, beginner-friendly guide to **Space Trader — Contracts**, a space trading game that runs in your web browser. There is no build step, no installation, and no account: you just open the page and play. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

The game is a sandbox. You are not forced down a single path. You can trade goods, take contracts, hunt criminals, fight pirates, upgrade your ship, and grow rich at your own pace. The only "end" is the one you choose: retire in comfort, or die out in the black.

## A one-paragraph summary of the game

You begin with a tiny ship, a small amount of money, and a dream. You fly from star system to star system, buying goods where they are cheap and selling them where they are expensive. Along the way you meet other ships — some friendly, some hostile, some lawful. You can fight, flee, bribe, or trade with them. You earn money to buy a bigger, tougher ship, equip it with weapons and shields, and take on contracts for extra rewards. The galaxy is dangerous and unpredictable, but it is also full of opportunity. How you play is entirely up to you.
