# 5. Ships & Equipment

Your ship is your home, your wallet on thrusters, and your lifeline. The **Ship** screen (the shipyard) is where you buy and sell ships, mount weapons and shields, install gadgets, refuel, and repair. This chapter covers everything you can bolt onto — or trade up to.

---

## Ship statistics, explained simply

Every ship is defined by a few numbers:

- **Cargo** — how many units of goods it can carry.
- **Weapons / Shields / Gadgets** — how many of each device it can mount.
- **Quarters** — crew space (used for hiring crew on the Dashboard).
- **Fuel** — how much fuel it holds (range between refuels).
- **Hull** — its structural health. At 0 hull the ship is destroyed.
- **Price** — what it costs to buy.
- **Fuel cost** — how expensive each unit of fuel is for this ship (also sets wormhole tolls).
- **Repair cost** — how much it costs to fix each point of hull damage.
- **Tech requirement** — the minimum tech level of a system where this ship is sold.

### Special ships

These vessels are **never offered at the shipyard**. You encounter them only as hostile raiders, disguised "traders," or as the targets of special missions. Their stats are listed for reference — you cannot buy or sell them, but knowing their strengths helps you survive an encounter.

| Ship | Cargo | Weapons | Shields | Gadgets | Fuel | Hull | Notes |
|------|-------|---------|---------|---------|------|------|-------|
| **Dragonfly** | 10 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 18 | 10 | A glass cannon: heavy shields but only 10 hull. Dies fast once shields drop. |
| **Mantis** | 35 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 15 | 300 | Tough raider with extra crew quarters. A frequent target of **hero** police missions. |
| **Scarab** | 20 | 2 | 0 | 2 | 12 | 400 | The tankiest raider (400 hull) but no shields — and it shrugs off most weapons. A slow brawler with cargo space. |
| **Scorp** | 20 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 14 | 250 | The best-armed raider (4 weapons). The ultimate hero-mission target. |

**Their peculiarities:**

- **Not purchasable.** None of them carry a price tag and they never appear in the shipyard list, regardless of tech level or reputation. They are part of the galaxy's hostile population, not its economy.
- **Event-driven.** They appear in combat and through special events. The **Mantis** and **Scorp** are specifically scripted as the destroyed targets of "hero" police missions (see chapter 8), so you will meet them on purpose as well as by chance.
- **Unusual builds.** Unlike buyable ships, these lean toward extremes: the Dragonfly hides behind shields with almost no hull, the Scarab soaks damage with 400 hull but zero shields, and the Scorp out-guns everything with 4 weapons.
- **Scarab armour quirk.** The Scarab's hull is shielded against almost every weapon: only the **Pulse laser** and **Morgan's laser** can damage it. Anything else — beams, military lasers, and the rest — simply bounces off. Bring one of those two weapons if you expect to fight a Scarab.
- **Disguised traders.** Some apparently friendly "traders" you encounter in deep space are actually these raiders in disguise, ready to turn on you once you are vulnerable.
- **Scaled threat.** The bigger and more valuable your own ship looks, the more interesting the encounters become (see chapter 7), drawing the attention of the tougher raiders. A small, cheap ship is less interesting to them.
- **No resale value.** Because they cannot be captured or traded, defeating one yields only bounty, reputation, and (sometimes) salvage from the combat itself — never a new hull for your fleet.

When you buy a new ship, your cargo, equipment, and fuel carry over as appropriate; you generally keep what you have installed.

## Weapons

Weapons determine how much damage you deal in combat. All weapons can be mounted on any ship that has a free weapon slot; more advanced ones simply cost more.

| Weapon | Price | Power |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Pulse Laser** | 2,000 | 15 |
| **Beam Laser** | 12,500 | 25 |
| **Military Laser** | 35,000 | 35 |
| **Morgan's Laser** | 50,000 | 85 |

Higher power means you blow through enemy shields and hull faster. A well-armed ship can intimidate or destroy almost anything.

---

## Shields

Shields absorb damage before it reaches your hull. While your shields hold, your hull is safe. When shields drop to zero, every hit damages the ship itself.

| Shield | Price | Power (absorption) |
|--------|-------|--------------------|
| **Energy Shield** | 5,000 | 100 |
| **Reflective Shield** | 20,000 | 200 |
| **Lightning Shield** | 45,000 | 350 |

Stacking multiple shields adds their absorption together, so a ship with two Energy Shields has 200 effective shield power. Shields also recharge between fights.

## Gadgets & Specials

Gadgets modify your ship and your skills. Specials are unique installations.

| Item | Price | Effect |
|------|-------|--------|
| **5 extra cargo bays** | 2,500 | +5 cargo space. A trader's best friend. |
| **Escape Pod** | 2,000 | Required for insurance; lets you survive ship destruction (see chapter 10). |
| **Auto-repair System** | 7,500 | +3 Engineer effectively; slowly fixes hull. |
| **Navigation System** | 15,000 | +3 Pilot effectively; easier escapes and better cloaking. |
| **Targeting System** | 25,000 | +3 Fighter effectively; better combat. |
| **Cloaking Device** | 100,000 | +2 Pilot and makes you harder to detect (invisibility). |
| **Fuel Compactor** | 30,000 | +18 fuel capacity (longer range). |
| **Renwick Retrofit Tech** | 50,000 | +50 hull strength (tougher ship). |

Gadgets and specials count against your ship's **gadget slots**, so a ship with only one gadget slot cannot hold them all. Choose based on your play style: traders want cargo bays, fighters want targeting and shields, explorers want navigation and fuel.

---

## Fuel and repairs

At the Ship screen you can also:

- **Refuel** — fill your tank. Cost depends on your ship's fuel cost per unit. You cannot travel without fuel.
- **Repair hull** — fix battle damage. Cost depends on your ship's repair cost per point. Never fly into danger with a damaged hull if you can avoid it.

Both can usually be done at any system's shipyard.

---

## Choosing your path

- **Pure trader:** maximize cargo (Beetle/Termite + cargo bays), keep combat minimal, avoid trouble.
- **Trader-fighter:** a balanced ship like the Grasshopper or Hornet with good shields and a couple of lasers.
- **Bounty hunter:** Wasp or Police cruiser, maxed weapons and shields, targeting system.

There is no wrong answer — only the ship that fits how you want to play.
