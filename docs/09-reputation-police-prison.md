# 9. Reputation, Police & Prison

How the galaxy perceives you matters as much as how much money you have. This chapter explains your two standing scores — **Reputation** and **Police Record** — and what happens when the law catches up with you.

---

## Reputation (how feared/respected you are in combat)

Your **Reputation** rises when you defeat enemies (pirates, raiders, mission targets) and falls when you fail or flee. It is a measure of your combat legend, independent of the law. The tiers are:

| Reputation | Title |
|-----------|-------|
| Very low | Harmless |
| | Mostly Harmless |
| | Poor |
| | Average |
| | Above Average |
| | Competent |
| | Dangerous |
| | Deadly |
| Highest | Elite |

Reputation is mostly a badge of honor and a score factor. High reputation signals an experienced fighter. You start at "Harmless."

---

## Police Record (how the law sees you)

Your **Police Record** tracks whether you are a law-abiding citizen or a wanted criminal. It starts at "Clean" and shifts based on your actions:

| Record | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Clean | Neutral starting point. |
| Lawful | You have been helpful/compliant. |
| Trusted | Reliably lawful — unlocks buying the Police cruiser. |
| Liked | Well regarded. |
| Hero | Exemplary; the pinnacle of good standing. |
| Dubious | Suspicious — sales start costing 10% more (black-market cut). |
| Crook | Clearly breaking laws. |
| Criminal | Wanted. |
| Villain | Dangerous offender. |
| Psycho | Worst possible — police may attack on sight. |

### What hurts your record
- Getting caught with **illegal goods** (Firearms, Narcotics) during a police inspection.
- **Attacking or killing police.**
- Fleeing a police inspection.
- Generally behaving like a pirate.

### What helps your record
- Completing **manhunt** and **hero** (police) missions.
- Complying with inspections when you have nothing to hide.
- Simply staying out of trouble.

Once your record drops to "Dubious" or below, every sale you make pays about **10% less**, because you are forced into the black market. A bad record is expensive as well as dangerous.

---

## Inspections

Police may stop you and inspect your cargo. If you are clean, nothing happens (and your record may even improve). If you carry illegal goods, you face:

- A **fine** (credits taken).
- **Confiscation** of the illegal cargo.
- A drop in your police record.

You can try to **bribe** your way out, but in strict governments this can fail and make things worse.

---

## Prison

If your crimes catch up with you, the law can throw you in **prison**. Being imprisoned costs you time and money — your record is set back, fines are levied, and you lose progress while locked up. It is a serious setback but not necessarily the end; once released you can rebuild.

The best defense is simple: keep your record clean, avoid illegal goods in policed systems, and never fire on the police unless you have fully committed to a life of crime.

---

## Living as a criminal vs. a law-abider

- **Law-abiding traders** enjoy full-price sales, easy police missions, and the ability to buy the Police cruiser. The downside is they must avoid illegal-goods profits.
- **Outlaws** can reap huge profits from Narcotics and Firearms in anarchic systems, but pay a sales penalty everywhere, face attack-on-sight police, and risk prison.

Neither is "wrong" — they are simply two different ways to play.
