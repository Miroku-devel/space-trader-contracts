# 4. Bank & Finance

Money is the fuel of your career, and the **Bank** is where you manage it. You can stash cash safely, borrow against your worth, and insure your ship. This chapter explains how.

---

## Deposits and withdrawals

You can keep your credits in the bank or carry them in your ship's account. Functionally the money is the same, but the bank is where loans and insurance live. There is no penalty for holding cash; the main reason to use the bank is to take a loan or buy insurance.

---

## Loans

Need more buying power than you have? The bank will lend you money **up to the value of your net worth** (your cash, ship, and cargo, minus any existing debt).

- **Interest:** loans accrue **10% interest per warp** (per jump between systems). The longer you travel without repaying, the more you owe.
- **Repayment:** you can repay any amount at any time from the Bank screen. Paying early saves interest.
- **Debt ceiling:** you cannot borrow beyond your net worth, and there is a hard ceiling. If your debt grows to **100,000 credits or more**, the bank flags your ship: **you are not allowed to leave the current system** until you bring the debt back down. A warning message is sent to your Mail when debt gets dangerously high.

Loans are a double-edged sword. Borrowing to buy a bigger ship or a lucrative cargo can multiply your income — but if a pirate strips you or prices move against you, the interest can snowball. New players should borrow cautiously.

---

## Insurance

The Bank also sells **ship insurance**. If your ship is destroyed and you have an escape pod (see chapter 5), insurance pays you the full value of the lost ship, softening the blow enormously.

- Insurance requires that you own an **Escape Pod**.
- It costs a small fee **per warp** (per jump). The fee is reduced by a "Safe Pilot Discount" tied to your skill.
- If you let your debt get too large, you cannot take out or maintain insurance.

For any pilot who flies into dangerous space, insurance is strongly recommended.

---

## Net worth

Your **net worth** is your cash plus the value of your ship and cargo, minus any loan debt. It is the number the Bank uses to decide how much it will lend you, and it is also a big part of your final **score** when you retire (chapter 10). Growing net worth is the real goal of the game.

---

## Practical tips

- Borrow only what a single good trade run can reasonably repay.
- Repay loans whenever you are sitting on a pile of trade profit.
- Always keep an Escape Pod and insurance once you can afford them — one unlucky fight should not end your career.
- Watch your Mail for debt warnings; ignore them at your peril.
