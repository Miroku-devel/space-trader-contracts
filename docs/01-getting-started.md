# 1. Getting Started

This chapter walks you through your very first moments in the game: the start screen, choosing your commander, understanding your skills, and learning the layout of the main screen.

## Creating your Commander

On the Commander screen you set up who you are:

- **Name** — type any name you like. This is your callsign for the rest of the game.
- **Avatar** — you can pick from a selection of portraits using the arrow buttons. This is purely cosmetic; it appears next to your name on the side panel.
- **Skills** — you have a pool of skill points to distribute among four abilities. Each skill can range from 1 to 10, and you must spend all your points before you can start.

You begin with a total of 20 skill points to spread across four skills, each starting at a minimum of 1. That means you choose how to specialize.

### The four skills

| Skill | What it helps with |
|-------|--------------------|
| **Pilot** | Flying, escaping from encounters, and using the Navigation System. Higher Pilot makes it easier to flee and improves cloaking. |
| **Fighter** | Combat accuracy and damage when using weapons. Higher Fighter makes you deadlier in a fight. |
| **Trader** | Getting better buy and sell prices at markets. Higher Trader means more profit on every trade. |
| **Engineer** | Repairing your ship and surviving malfunctions. Higher Engineer reduces repair costs and helps auto-repair. |

If you are unsure, a balanced start works well. If you want to focus, consider leaning into **Trader** early (more money) or **Fighter/Pilot** (more survivability). You can also press **Random** to let the game assign your points.

When you are happy, press **Start Game**. You are now in the galaxy.

---

## Your starting situation

You begin the game with:

- A **Flea** — the smallest, weakest ship available.
- A small amount of starting cash (1,000 credits).
- A full tank of fuel.
- A clean police record and a "Harmless" reputation.

There is no timer counting down and no forced quest. You are free to explore.

---

## The main screen

The game world is a star map. Most of the screen is the galaxy view, showing hundreds of stars. Around the edge you will find your controls:

- A **side information panel** showing your name, credits, location, ship status, police record, and reputation.
- A **bottom bar (HUD)** of large buttons, one for each activity:

| Button | Opens |
|--------|-------|
| **Home** | Returns the map view to your current location. |
| **Trade** | The market of the system you are currently in. |
| **Search** | A price scanner that shows where goods are cheap or expensive nearby. |
| **Dashboard** | Your personal status: skills, crew, time played, and retirement. |
| **Ship** | The shipyard: buy/sell ships, equipment, fuel, and repairs. |
| **Bank** | Deposits, loans, and insurance. |
| **Travel** | Begin a journey to the selected star. |
| **Wormhole** | Use a wormhole if you are standing on one. |
| **Route** | Plan a multi-stop path across the galaxy. |
| **Mail** | Messages, contract offers, and news from the galaxy. |
| **Mission** | Your current contracts and available new ones. |
| **Options** | Settings, new game, and reset. |

On a phone or tablet, the same buttons appear as a footer bar. The game works on both desktop and mobile.

---

## Your first few actions (suggested)

1. Open **Trade** and look at the prices of the ten goods in your starting system.
2. Open **Search** to see which nearby systems sell those goods cheaply or buy them dearly.
3. Buy a few units of the cheapest good, then open **Route** or **Travel** to fly somewhere that pays more.
4. Sell at a profit, repeat, and watch your credits grow.
5. Visit the **Ship** screen to refuel and, later, to upgrade.

Everything else in this manual explains the systems behind those simple steps.
