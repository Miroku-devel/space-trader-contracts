# 6. Traveling Between Systems

The galaxy is vast, and getting from one star to another is what the whole game is built around. This chapter covers the map, selecting destinations, fuel, routes, and the mysterious wormholes.

---

## The star map

The main view is an interactive star map of all 800 systems. You can:

- **Pan** by dragging.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel or pinch gestures.
- **Click a star** to select it as your destination and see its info (tech level, government, status, distance).

Stars you have visited are marked. Wormholes and special locations look different from ordinary stars. You can also **bookmark** favorite systems with a star icon so you can find them again.

---

## Selecting and traveling

1. Click the star you want to visit. The info panel shows how far it is (in parsecs) and what kind of place it is.
2. Press **Travel** to begin the journey.

Travel takes a moment of animation (you can usually skip or speed it up). When you arrive, time advances by one **warp** (a "day"/turn), loan interest ticks, and random events may occur.

### Fuel

Every jump consumes fuel based on distance. When your tank runs low you must **refuel** at a shipyard before you can go far. Running out of fuel far from anywhere is a real risk in a small ship, so watch your gauge. The **Fuel Compactor** gadget and larger ships give you more range.

---

## Planning a route

The **Route** button lets you chain several stars into a planned path. Instead of flying one hop at a time, you set a sequence of destinations and the map shows your planned course. This is handy for long trade circuits: visit the cheap buyer, then the expensive seller, then home, all in one plan.

---

## Wormholes

Scattered through the galaxy are **wormholes** — special stars that act as instantaneous gateways. A wormhole connects to exactly one other system (its exit). Flying into a wormhole does not consume normal fuel the way a long journey does, but it charges a **toll** in credits based on your ship (cheaper ships pay less). Wormholes let you cross huge distances in a single jump, which is invaluable for reaching distant, lucrative markets.

### The single-way wormhole mystery

Beyond the ordinary linked wormholes, the galaxy hides a special **one-way wormhole** puzzle. Finding and solving it is an advanced secret: it involves discovering a special cargo crate, ferrying it to the right place, and completing a small sequence puzzle at the wormhole scene. Success opens a path to a unique destination — a **Habitable Moon** — that normal travel cannot reach. Treat this as an end-game exploration goal; you do not need it to enjoy the game, but it rewards the curious.

---

## Habitable Moons

Very rarely, a system contains a **Habitable Moon** — a special colony. These are tied to the wormhole secret and to unique mail messages. They are points of interest for explorers rather than routine trade stops.

---

## Practical travel advice

- Keep your fuel above what a return trip would cost.
- Use **Route** to visualize long loops before committing.
- Use **Wormholes** to shortcut across the map, but remember the toll.
- Check the destination's status and government before you fly — some systems are far more dangerous than others (chapter 7 and 9).
